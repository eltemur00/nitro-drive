Game Overview

Nitro Drive: Retro Arcade is a simple arcade racing game where the player controls a car and avoids incoming enemy cars. The player uses the left and right keys to move across the road while trying to survive as long as possible and achieve the highest score.

The game is designed to be simple, fun, and easy to understand for players who enjoy classic arcade games.

Game Information

  • Game Name: Nitro Drive
  • Genre: Racing / Arcade
  • Players: Single Player
  • Objective: Get the highest score

Story

In the world of Nitro Drive, every drive is a challenge. You are a driver entering a retro arcade race where the road never stops and obstacles constantly appear ahead.

Your goal is simple: stay on the road, avoid the incoming cars, and keep driving for as long as you can. Every second is a chance to increase your score and beat the high score.

Characters

Gameplay

How to Play

  1. Start the game.
  2. Control the racing car.
  3. Avoid obstacles.
  4. Collect points.
  5. Try to survive as long as possible.
  6. Get the highest score.

Controls

  • Left Arrow - Move Left
  • Right Arrow - Move Right
